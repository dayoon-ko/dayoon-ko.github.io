ScholarCatalyst is a literature inspiration retrieval benchmark grounded in researchers' firsthand knowledge of their own projects. 184 researchers who led 207 recent computer science projects verified 894 research questions, labeling which prior papers did or could have advanced their work. The benchmark covers 207 source papers, 191k candidate documents, and 894 queries (207 core queries and 687 subfield queries).
