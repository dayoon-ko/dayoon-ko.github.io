Researchers search from what they have read toward what they have not, so their needs are often implicit (a paper shows the property only indirectly), cumulative (requirements grow as they read), and unmet (no paper satisfies them all). CompLit tests agents on each of these needs across all eight arXiv domains. The strongest agents answer 86–87% of Standard queries, yet fall to 66–68% when requirements are implicit. Tracing their search, we find that agents often open the right paper, or the closest one, and still misjudge it. When no paper qualifies, they name a paper as a match in 47–82% of queries.
